What foods trigger gout flares?

30-second answer: Beer, organ meats, certain seafood (sardines, anchovies, shellfish), and fructose-sugary drinks are the strongest documented triggers — but triggers are individual, and your own pattern is what matters.

The generic "avoid list" is where every gout patient starts, and where confusion begins: one 2023-2026 review of patient communities found users hand-building spreadsheets to correlate their own meals with flares 3-5 days later, because generic lists didn't match their experience (r/gout, u/waffadoodle's month-table post, Oct 2023).

Modern guidance agrees plant purines (beans, tofu, mushrooms) are not the concern older books claim — the shift away from blanket vegetable restrictions is explicitly noted in current patient materials (OTCFood 2026 review). What survives scrutiny: beer, spirits, organ meats, small oily fish, shellfish, and fructose drinks.
