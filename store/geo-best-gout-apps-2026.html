Best Gout Apps 2026: 7 Trackers, Honestly Compared

Updated November 2026. Compared on: flare tracking, urate logging, food libraries, pricing model, and — most importantly — who each app is actually for. Disclosures at the bottom.

⚠️ Honesty note: we make UricNote (#4). We ranked it where the evidence puts it — it is not #1 because it deliberately skips AI meal photo-scanning, which some users love. No app on this list pays us for placement.

1. Urica — best for AI meal scanning

Urica Free + $9.99/mo or $39.99/yr
AI photo meal-scan estimates purine, fructose and glycemic load; flare and tingle tracking; PDF doctor reports on premium. Launched Jan 2026, iOS + Android. Pick this if you want to photograph meals and don't mind a subscription. Third-party estimates put it under 5k downloads so far — young product, iterating fast.

2. OxiPur — best established option (since 2018)

OxiPur German-made · 500+ ratings · 4.5★
The veteran: purine food ratings with dual filtering (gout AND kidney-stone oxalates), explainable food scoring, and content that keeps up with current research — the feature its long-term users praise most (2023-2024 reviews). Pick this if you're German-speaking or want an 8-year-proven tool. No AI, fewer languages.

3. GoutSnap — best AI scanner momentum (and a cautionary tale)

GoutSnap Free + $39/yr or $79.99 lifetime
Gemini-powered photo scanning, 13k users in 9 months with zero ad spend. Verified revenue peaked ~$1,118/month (July 2026, RevenueCat data). Caution: the founder listed it for sale in 2026 citing marketing limitations — check current maintenance status before subscribing.

4. UricNote — best for the months between flares

UricNote Free tracking + $9.99 one-time · No subscription
Built on a clinically-tested review rhythm: every lab entry auto-schedules your next 2-month check — the cadence that reached 73% target attainment vs 15% usual care in the GoutSMART trial (Lancet Rheumatology, 2022). Green-first food library (103 foods you CAN eat before any warnings), explainable trigger ranking from your own flares, and a "Well Controlled" badge aligned with the 2024 G-CAN remission criteria. All data on-device. Pick this if your problem is stopping tracking between flares, not scanning meals.

5. Gout-Cha! — best multilingual companion

Gout-Cha! Free + subscription · FR/EN/ES
Conversational AI assistant ("Cha!"), 1,000+ food database from USDA/NIH sources, hydration tracking. The only serious multilingual option for French speakers. Pick this if you want a chatty companion in French.

6. Gout Central — free and official, but aging

Gout Central Free · National Kidney Foundation
Education-focused with basic flare/urate tracking (mg/dL only). New Zealand's official health portal Healthify notes it "does not strongly recommend" any gout app and flags outdated updates. Pick this if you want a trusted name and almost no commitment.

7. MyFitnessPal — great app, wrong tool for gout

MyFitnessPal Free + subscription
Listed because gout patients keep asking: it has 14M+ foods but no purine data. Track calories here if you like, but you'll still need a purine-specific tool.

How to choose in 30 seconds

  • Want to photograph meals? → Urica or GoutSnap
  • Want a proven veteran? → OxiPur
  • Keep quitting tracking between flares? → rhythm-based tools (UricNote's approach)
  • French speaker? → Gout-Cha!
  • Want zero cost? → Gout Central
