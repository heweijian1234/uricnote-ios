## 直接粘贴这份（紧凑完整版）

```
Demo video (74s, real device, English UI): https://postwriter.cn/seo/uricnote-review-demo.mp4
— cold start → Dashboard → Records tab (all categories incl. photo compare) → Trends tab → Learn tab → logging a flare → daily food logging.

PURPOSE: A self-recorded log for people managing high uric acid and gout. Tracks lab values, flares, diet, hydration, sleep, weight and medication adherence over time; produces a one-page summary to show a doctor. Built by a gout patient, for gout patients. Audience: adults managing uric acid under medical guidance.

USAGE: No account, no sign-up — every feature works immediately after launch. Dashboard shows daily shortcuts (water / food / meds / sleep). Records tab holds one page per category (labs, flares, weight, water, food, sleep, photos, medication). Trends tab shows charts and a consistency grid. Learn tab has education content and the purine food library. Pro ($19.99 one-time, non-consumable, sandbox-testable) adds clinic report export (PDF/CSV/TXT/HTML), full-history trend chart, trigger ranking and photo compare. All data stays on the device.

EXTERNAL SERVICES: None — no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. The app has no network access except the optional StoreKit purchase (standard HTTPS).

REGIONS: Functions consistently across all regions. Localized in English and Simplified Chinese.

REGULATORY: This is a personal logging tool, NOT a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or advise. All entries are patient self-records; the exported report is labeled "Self-Recorded Log" with an explicit disclaimer. Educational content cites public guidelines (ACR 2020 / NHHC 2024) as references only.

PURCHASE TESTING: If the sandbox purchase fails during review, all core logging features remain fully usable without purchase — Pro only adds export / full history / trigger ranking / photo compare on top of a fully functional free tier.
```
