## 表单内容

**Tell us about your app:**
```
UricNote is a gout and uric-acid record keeper built on a treat-to-target
review rhythm validated in clinical research (GoutSMART, Lancet Rheumatology
2022: 2-monthly supported self-management reached 73% target attainment vs
15% usual care).

What makes it nomination-worthy:
1. PRIVACY-FIRST, VERIFIABLY: zero network access, no account, no analytics.
   Health data never leaves the device — independently checkable (the app
   binary makes no connections).
2. BUILT BY A PATIENT: the solo developer lives with high uric acid himself
   and ships from China to a global audience using cloud CI on Windows.
3. A GENTLER PATTERN FOR CHRONIC CARE: "green-first" food browsing shows
   patients what they CAN eat before any restriction — a response to the
   documented emotional burden of chronic-diet apps (28% of gout patients
   report depression; Gout Education Society, May 2026).
4. CLINICALLY GROUNDED GAMIFICATION: the "Well Controlled" badge aligns
   with the G-CAN 2024 clinical remission criteria — motivation that maps
   to real medicine.

No subscription (one-time unlock), no ads, 7-language localization out of
the box (EN/JA/DE/FR/ES/zh-Hans/zh-Hant).
```

**What's the story behind this app?**
```
I was diagnosed with high uric acid and put on daily medication. I tried
every tracker — they were either subscription paywalls, ad-heavy, or
designed for someone else's condition. I also kept forgetting why tracking
mattered between flares. So I built the app I wanted: a clinical rhythm,
green-first food guidance, and a doctor report that fits a 15-minute visit.
The 63%-misconception statistic (patients believing diet alone is enough)
came from my own community's discussions — the app shows both tracks
(medication and lifestyle) side by side so data settles the argument.
```

**Which platform/iOS feature should we know about?**
```
Local notifications power the medication and review-rhythm reminders — the
core clinical loop works fully offline. SwiftData local persistence; no
HealthKit yet (deliberately: keeping the v1.0 privacy surface minimal).
```
