What should my uric acid target be?

30-second answer: Below 6.0 mg/dL (360 µmol/L). Below 5.0 mg/dL (300 µmol/L) if you have tophi or frequent attacks.

Only 40% of diagnosed patients know their urate level (Gout Education Society national survey, May 2026, n=1,250) — knowing your number and your target is step one of treat-to-target management. Watch units: US labs use mg/dL, most of Europe µmol/L, New Zealand mmol/L (0.36 = 6.0 mg/dL). A tracker that auto-converts prevents the most common "is my number good?" confusion.

Sources: ACR 2020 Guideline; GES/Wakefield survey May 2026; Healthify NZ unit guidance (2026).
