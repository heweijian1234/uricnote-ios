Short answer:Limit strictly — portion matters most at this level. Avoid during flares; even in remission, keep frequency low. Distilled spirits contain almost zero purines — but for gout the risk comes from the alcohol itself (alcohol slows uric acid excretion). "Zero purine" does not mean "safe to drink".

What this means

The goal in gout and hyperuricemia management is keeping serum uric acid below 6.0 mg/dL (360 µmol/L) (ACR 2020 guideline; below 5.0 if you have tophi). Diet accounts for roughly 30% — baijiu (chinese liquor) falls in the strictly-limited category, but medication and lifestyle must run on parallel tracks: 63% of patients mistakenly believe diet alone can replace daily medication (US national survey, Gout Education Society, May 2026).
