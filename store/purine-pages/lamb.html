What this means

The goal in gout and hyperuricemia management is keeping serum uric acid below 6.0 mg/dL (360 µmol/L) (ACR 2020 guideline; below 5.0 if you have tophi). Diet accounts for roughly 30% — lamb falls in the moderation category, but medication and lifestyle must run on parallel tracks: 63% of patients mistakenly believe diet alone can replace daily medication (US national survey, Gout Education Society, May 2026).
