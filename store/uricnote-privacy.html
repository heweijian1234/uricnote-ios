UricNote — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Short version: UricNote keeps all your health data on your device. We collect nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

1. What data the app stores

UricNote stores your entries — lab values, flare records, medication plans and logs, hydration, weight, and food notes — only on your device, using Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults/JSON). Nothing is uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere.

2. What we collect

Nothing. UricNote has no network access, no analytics SDKs, no third-party code that phones home. Deleting the app deletes all data permanently.

3. Health data

UricNote does not connect to HealthKit and does not write to iCloud. Your entries are never used for advertising, data mining, or any purpose beyond displaying them back to you (and the doctor report you choose to generate and share yourself).

4. Doctor report export

When you generate a doctor report, the document is created on your device and shared only through Apple's standard share sheet, under your control. We never see it.

5. Feedback email

If you choose to send feedback, you use your own email app — we receive only what you choose to write. It is used solely to improve the app (and, when users agree, credited in the changelog).

6. Future changes

If a future version ever adds an optional cloud feature, this policy will be updated before release, and data will never leave your device without your explicit, informed action.
