Does beer really cause flares more than other alcohol?

30-second answer: Beer yes — strongest documented association. But all alcohol raises urate, so total reduction beats only-quitting-beer.

Beer's extra effect likely comes from guanosine, a plant purine specific to beer, stacked on alcohol's own urate-raising mechanism. Spirit drinkers are not exempt: ethanol itself slows uric acid excretion regardless of the drink. The practical target is reducing total alcohol, gradually — which is also more sustainable than cliff-edge quitting (behavior-design evidence, Fogg 2019).
