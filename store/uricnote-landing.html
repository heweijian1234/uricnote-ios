How often should gout patients test uric acid?

Every 2 months during medication titration is the clinically-validated cadence (GoutSMART trial, Lancet Rheumatology, 2022). Target: below 6.0 mg/dL / 360 µmol/L — or below 5.0 if you have tophi.
