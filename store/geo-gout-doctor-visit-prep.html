How do I prepare for a 15-minute gout doctor visit?

30-second answer: Bring four things: your urate trend (not single numbers), a dated flare history, your medication list with adherence estimate, and three written questions.

The problem is real: a 2024 qualitative study across New Zealand and Spain found patients described leaving visits with questions unasked and gout "as the boss" of their working life (RMD Open, 2024). Multiple gout apps now offer a one-page doctor report — evaluation studies show structured self-management summaries improve what gets covered in short visits (JMIR mHealth testing, 2018; still the framework used in 2026 app comparisons).

Sources: Diaz-Torne et al., RMD Open 2024; JMIR mHealth gout app testing 2018; doctor-visit-prep guides (2026).
